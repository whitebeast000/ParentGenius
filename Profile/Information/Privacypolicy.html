Privacy Policy

Condition & Attending

Effective Date: July 30, 2025

At ParentGenius, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use,
protect, and share your personal information when you use our website, app, and related 
services (“Services”).
1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
a.
Account Information
Name, email address, and password
Child age ranges and parenting goals (to personalize content)
b.
Usage Data
Course and content activity
Progress tracking and earned rewards
Interactions in the community or forums
Device information and app usage statistics
c.
Optional Data (if you choose to provide)
Survey responses
Shared solutions or success stories
Therapy or coaching intake forms (if booked)
d.
Payment Information
We do not store payment details directly. Payments are securely processed through trusted 
third-party services (e.g., Stripe, Apple/Google In-App Payments).
2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
Provide and improve the app experience
Personalize content based on your parenting needs
Track your progress and rewards
Recommend relevant courses or solutions
Facilitate customer support and feedback
Send emails or notifications (which you can opt out of)
3. Sharing Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only:
With your consent (e.g., if you join a group or comment publicly)
With service providers helping us run the platform (e.g., hosting, analytics)
If required by law or to protect our legal rights
With therapists or coaches you choose to connect with
4. Data Security
We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no 
system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and avoid sharing personal 
login credentials.
5. Your Choices
You can:
Access or update your account at any time
Delete your account and data by emailing support@parentgenius.com
Opt out of marketing emails
Choose whether or not to engage with community or coaching features
6. Children’s Privacy
ParentGenius is designed for parents and caregivers, not children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect data from children.
7. International Users
If you are accessing our services from outside the U.S., your data may be transferred and stored 
in the United States. By using our services, you consent to this transfer.
8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will 
notify you of any material changes via the app or email.
